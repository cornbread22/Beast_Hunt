How to Play Beast Hunt

Goal

Find the beast using the clues, then shoot it from an adjacent tile. Touching a hole or the beast, or firing in the wrong direction, ends the game.

Clues

  • A red center means the beast is within 2 moves.
  • A green ring means a hole is within 1 move.
  • Clues are revealed when you visit a tile and remain visible afterward.
  • Unvisited tiles and visited safe tiles have different backgrounds.

Legend

  • Red center: beast within 2 moves
  • Green ring: hole within 1 move
  • Unvisited tile
  • Visited safe tile
  • Your position
  • ≈River: carries you to the opposite bank

Controls and board

  • On desktop, use the arrow keys to move and aim.
  • On touch devices, swipe the board to move. While aiming, swipe to change direction.
  • Touch devices can also use the on-screen arrows.
  • The board wraps around when you move past an edge.
  • Enter a curved river to teleport to its opposite bank.
  • Your arrow flies toward the adjacent tile. A missed shot or bumping into the beast triggers its fire-breathing cutscene.
  • Select Shoot, choose a direction, then select Fire arrow.
  • The beast must be on the immediately adjacent tile in the direction you shoot.
  • Select Restart game at any time to start a new hunt.

Difficulty

  • Easy: fewer holes; holes are at least 3 moves apart.
  • Normal: more holes than before; holes are at least 2 moves apart.
  • Hard: many holes that can be next to each other.
  • Impossible: a dense field of holes, including beside your starting tile.
  • Map size: choose a 7 x 7, 9 x 9, or 11 x 11 board. Larger boards contain more holes; small maps have one river while medium and large maps have two.
  • Separate rivers are placed so they do not touch, including across wrapped edges.

High scores

The online boards rank the top 10 wins by fewest moves for each difficulty and map size. Daily scores use the UTC calendar day; overall scores keep each player's all-time best.
